Model assumptions

Assumes max weapon skill, 3/3 Aggression, and 2/2 Efficient Strikes. Enter your sheet AP, hit, and expertise, and your crit before the weapon-specific Hack and Slash bonus. The default 0 armour is a reference target.

Each fight starts at 100 Energy and regenerates 10 per second. Sinister Strike requires 40 Energy to cast. A miss, dodge, or parry refunds 32 Energy in this model. The one-second global cooldown still applies. First swings occur after one weapon interval.

The model uses Classic-style estimates for armour, glancing blows, and melee attack chances against an equal-level target. Sinister Strike uses 1.7-second normalization for daggers and 2.4 seconds for other one-handed weapons.

Hack and Slash adds 5 percentage points of crit to attacks from daggers and fist weapons. Sinister Strike uses the main hand’s bonus. Landed sword and axe attacks have a 5% chance to grant a main-hand swing in the model. Extra swings use the normal main-hand hit table and leave the regular swing timer in place. Each eligible hit rolls independently, and extra swings do not trigger another extra swing.

A main-hand mace reduces target armour for both hands and Sinister Strike. Use 15% for the talent tooltip or about 9% to reproduce the measured effect on Saltwater Snapjaws. The switches for an off-hand mace or off-hand sword or axe let you test the unverified cases. Off-hand swings default to 75% damage for 2/2 Dual Wield Specialization.

This test covers weapon swings, Sinister Strike, and Hack and Slash. Item procs such as Thrash Blade, poisons, Poised, Shank, finishers, buffs, and target death are outside its scope. Research and sources
