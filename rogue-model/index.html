Model assumptions

Level 29 Rogue with max weapon skill, 3/3 Aggression, and 2/2 Efficient Strikes. Enter total sheet stats, including gear and talents. The default 0 armour is a reference target.

Each fight starts at 100 Energy and regenerates 10 per second. Sinister Strike costs 40 Energy and fires whenever available. An avoided strike uses the full cost in this test. First swings occur after one weapon interval.

The model uses Classic-style estimates for armour, glancing blows, and melee attack chances against an equal-level target. It uses a 2.4-second normalization speed and a 50% off-hand damage multiplier. These assumptions still need validation with Forever combat logs.

This test covers weapon swings and Sinister Strike. Poisons, Poised, Shank, finishers, buffs, and target death are outside its scope. Research and sources
