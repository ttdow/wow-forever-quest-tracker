# Lower Blackrock Spire

**Lower Blackrock Spire (55–60)** appears under Dungeons. The recommended range and five-player size come from the Lower wing of the current Blackrock Spire registry, map **229**. The November 27, 2026 review compares the Lower wing guide, individual quest pages, QuestieDB, player kill records, and the wing assignments in All The Things. [Lower wing guide](https://foreverchanges.pro/dungeons/lower-blackrock-spire) · [Dungeon registry](https://foreverdb.net/data/dungeons/229.json) · [Candidate decisions](sources.json)

The 17 quest entries cover Put Her Down, both Kibler pet quests, Mother's Milk, Urok Doomhowl, the Seal of Ascension, Bijou and Maxwell's Alliance route, Wyrmthalak's letter, the Mosh'aru tablets, and the relevant Blacksmithing and cross-dungeon tasks. The list uses the Alliance/shared and Rogue eligibility rules and excludes seasonal quests. Upper-only objectives such as Doomrigger's Clasp and General Drakkisath's Demise belong to Upper Spire. The two Mor Grayhoof quests are assigned to class branches that exclude Rogues. [Wing and class records](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/3b5a8ce4def64a4277b670c3f62c5954f679b325/.contrib/.db/forever/dungeons%20%26%20raids/blackrock%20spire.lua)

Both Seal of Ascension steps start with Vaelan, disguised as the Scarshield Infiltrator. The first step names all three gemstone sources and the Unadorned Seal. The second sends the player to Emberstrife in Dustwallow Marsh to forge it. The Final Tablets links to the God Hakkar chain in Sunken Temple through The Lost Tablets of Mosh'aru and leads to Confront Yeh'kinya in Stranglethorn Vale. The four reused IDs share completion with Winterspring and Blackrock Depths. [Quest data](../../data/quests.json)

Drops includes 66 pieces of equipment across 17 expandable cards. It covers the nine regular encounters, seven rares, and summoned Mor Grayhoof. Urok's card identifies the summon. The Rogue filter shows 43 items, taking tooltip class restrictions into account for pieces such as Ironweave Bracers and Ironweave Belt. Current tooltips explicitly name all 66 sources, and 39 of those associations also appear in player reports. The Ironweave Belt's tooltip and the addon both name Mor Grayhoof. A player report groups one copy with Voone, so the tracker keeps the tooltip source.
