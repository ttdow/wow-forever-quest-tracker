# Alcaz Prison

Open **Dungeons → Alcaz Prison** for the quest checklist, five boss drop tables, and a two-section route map. The dungeon is on Alcaz Island in Dustwallow Marsh. Its suggested level range is 48–53, and the Forever client records it as instance 2994 and area 16620. [Blizzard dungeon overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301455/) · [Forever client encounters](https://wago.tools/db2/DungeonEncounter/csv?build=1.60.1.71037)

## Quest coverage

- **Theramore investigation** starts with **Our Man in Theramore**, **Watch Yer Back**, and **The Return of the King?**. Sully then offers **A Bit of Wet Work** and **The Secret Prison**. The Perfumed Note leads to **Somber News**, followed by **Shaw Needs To Know**. Stormwind and Theramore pickup coordinates follow the current Forever NPC tooltips and the addon. [Quest and coordinate records](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f79803332c8e5eefc2b5840c4ce9efa17880a304/.contrib/.db/forever/dungeons%20%26%20raids/alcaz%20prison.lua) · [Forever quest guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/alcaz-prison-dungeon-overview-location-rewards)
- **The Dragon's Journal** starts with Archmage Lan'dalock in Dalaran, Alterac Mountains, at 15.4, 73.8. The quest is level 53 and can be accepted at level 46. Blazeroar drops Zarla's Journal. [Quest record](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96772) · [Giver coordinates](https://nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/npc/20735)
- **Mason Connery** offers **The Man in the Iron Chains** near the final encounter if The Questioner is still alive. Accept the quest, then return upstairs to kill The Questioner and recover the key. **More Valuable Than Gold** leads to the loose floorboards and Connery's Trunk upstairs in the Moonbrook inn in Westfall at about 43.4, 67.8. The Document Box has two Alliance-accessible **State Secrets** hand-ins. Renzik in Stormwind is Alliance-only and Rix Xizzix in Booty Bay is shared. The quests have distinct IDs and are alternative destinations for the same box. [Mason’s quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96335) · [Document Box](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Document_Box)

The 12 tracked quests include Alliance and shared entries. The separate Horde chains and the Nazgrel version of State Secrets are documented in the [source inventory](sources.json). Quest links use actual quest IDs and prerequisite links use the pinned addon’s records. Interior quest pickups use area names because precise game coordinates are unavailable.

## Drops

The tracker lists 15 encounter-specific equipment drops, three each from Tidemistress Ca'zass, Overseer Wrex, Katerina the Cleaner, Blazeroar, and The Questioner. Zarla Ober and Blazeroar are phases of one encounter. Nine items pass the Rogue usability filter. All 15 source lines and item stats are present in the Forever item tooltips. PatchVault also records 12 of those items from player kills. Keys, quest items, quest rewards, and shared zone drops are outside the boss equipment list. [Tooltip records](item-tooltips.json) · [Player-recorded drops](https://patchvaultapp.com/forever/dungeons/alcaz-prison/)

## Map

The **Main Unit** has pins for the entrance, the naga and Defias wings, The Questioner's side room, and the stairs. The **Maximum Security Unit** leads from the stairs to Katerina and Blazeroar, with Mason and the Perfumed Note near the final encounter. These are original route diagrams based on the described layout. Pin positions are approximate diagram coordinates. The Map footer links Santiago Reyes's detailed floor plans on Method. [Route and room descriptions](https://mygameacademia.com/forever/dungeon/alcaz-prison) · [Method floor plans](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/dungeons/alcaz-prison#dungeon-map)
