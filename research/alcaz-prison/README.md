# Alcaz Prison

Open **Dungeons → Alcaz Prison** for the quest checklist, five boss drop tables, a bind-on-equip drop table, and maps of both floors. The dungeon is on Alcaz Island in Dustwallow Marsh. Its suggested level range is 48–53, and the Forever client records it as instance 2994 and area 16620. [Blizzard dungeon overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301455/) · [Forever client encounters](https://wago.tools/db2/DungeonEncounter/csv?build=1.60.1.71037)

## Quest coverage

- **Theramore investigation** starts with **Our Man in Theramore**, **Watch Yer Back**, and **The Return of the King?**. Sully then offers **A Bit of Wet Work** and **The Secret Prison**. The Perfumed Note leads to **Somber News**, followed by **Shaw Needs To Know**. Stormwind and Theramore pickup coordinates follow the current Forever NPC tooltips and the addon. [Quest and coordinate records](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f79803332c8e5eefc2b5840c4ce9efa17880a304/.contrib/.db/forever/dungeons%20%26%20raids/alcaz%20prison.lua) · [Forever quest guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/alcaz-prison-dungeon-overview-location-rewards)
- **The Dragon's Journal** starts with Archmage Lan'dalock in Dalaran, Alterac Mountains, at 15.4, 73.8. The quest is level 53 and can be accepted at level 46. Blazeroar drops Zarla's Journal. [Quest record](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96772) · [Giver coordinates](https://nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/npc/20735)
- **Mason Connery** offers **The Man in the Iron Chains** near the final encounter if The Questioner is still alive. Accept the quest, then return upstairs to kill The Questioner and recover the key. **More Valuable Than Gold** leads to the loose floorboards and Connery's Trunk upstairs in the Moonbrook inn in Westfall at about 43.4, 67.8. The Document Box has two Alliance-accessible **State Secrets** hand-ins. Renzik in Stormwind is Alliance-only and Rix Xizzix in Booty Bay is shared. The quests have distinct IDs and are alternative destinations for the same box. [Mason’s quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96335) · [Document Box](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Document_Box)

The 12 tracked quests include Alliance and shared entries. The separate Horde chains and the Nazgrel version of State Secrets are documented in the [source inventory](sources.json). Quest links use actual quest IDs and prerequisite links use the pinned addon’s records. Interior quest pickups use area names because precise game coordinates are unavailable.

## Drops

The tracker lists 15 encounter-specific equipment drops, three each from Tidemistress Ca'zass, Overseer Wrex, Katerina the Cleaner, Blazeroar, and The Questioner. Zarla Ober and Blazeroar are phases of one encounter. Nine of these items pass the Rogue usability filter. All 15 source lines and item stats are present in the Forever item tooltips. PatchVault also records 12 of those items from player kills. [Tooltip records](item-tooltips.json) · [Player-recorded drops](https://patchvaultapp.com/forever/dungeons/alcaz-prison/)

The **Alcaz Prison BoE drops** card lists ten dungeon-specific items, including three pieces of Defias Enforcer's Garb. Seven pass the Rogue filter. All The Things lists individual mobs for nine items, and ForeverChanges lists Escape Plan among the dungeon's trash drops. The tooltip for Enchantresses' Spire also names the Strashaz Enchantress. [Source inventory](sources.json) · [Tooltip records](item-tooltips.json)

## Map

Both floor plans are by **Santiago Reyes** for **Atlas de Azeroth: Forever**. The Main Unit uses the full-resolution copy of the supplied Reddit image. The Maximum Security Unit uses the matching image from the artist's gallery. Each 3000 × 2000 JPEG is stored unmodified with its original watermarks and credits intact. [Reddit release](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/1xw4new/oc_i_have_created_all_the_dungeon_maps_for_phase/) · [Artist's gallery](https://sreyesmaps.com/mapas/la-prision-de-alcaz) · [Artwork usage terms](https://sreyesmaps.com/post/proyecto-atlas-de-azeroth-forever)
