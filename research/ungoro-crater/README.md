# Un'Goro Crater

The tracker lists **66 Alliance and shared quests** under **Zones → Un'Goro Crater (48–55)**. The suggested range reflects when the main leveling quests open and where most of them end. The list includes earlier pickups, later chain stages, and seasonal and repeatable quests with their own requirements. Reviewed November 27, 2026 against the current quest pages and QuestieDB's Forever data. [Quest data](../../data/quests.json) · [Sources and candidate decisions](sources.json)

The **Map** tab links 54 tracked quests and shows rare locations, the Ornate Chest, and gathering sites. [Un'Goro map sources and coverage](../ungoro-crater-map/README.md)

The review accounts for 86 candidates from the 54-entry main index, the 47-entry secondary index, the 61-entry API index, and the primary zone, starter, and objective records. It includes 51 quests from the main index and 15 additional tasks and handoffs. Those additions cover the Alliance Soil and Morrowgrain sequence, the Gorishi chain, Super Sticky, the Un'Goro step of A Little Slime Goes a Long Way, the Alliance Fire Plume Forged step, Elder Thunderhorn, and two multi-zone objectives.
