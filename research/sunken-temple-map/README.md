# Sunken Temple map

Choose **Dungeons → Sunken Temple → Map** for 67 searchable entries. The three indoor map images are stitched from the Forever client's minimap tiles. Four views separate the overlapping entrance, upper balconies, lower statues, and main hall levels. The fifth view shows the approach on the client map of the Swamp of Sorrows. [Client minimaps and dimensions](https://foreverdb.net/data/dungeons/109.json) · [Map data](../../data/maps/sunken-temple.json)

## Inside the temple

- **Main halls and dragons** marks Jammal'an, Ogom, the four drakes, the Shade of Eranikus, the Essence Font, and the Hakkar event in its western chamber. The four flames are marked separately. Morphaz's pin links **The Azure Key**. Malfurion's pin is a scripted Classic reference for the Green Scepter Shard chain. Its present availability in Forever is unverified. Elder Starsong is seasonal. The central pool art is visible below the higher walkways on this stitched image. [Quest and loot research](../sunken-temple/README.md)
- **Entrance and outer ring** uses the portal arrival from Classic area trigger 446. Spawn of Hakkar's marker is the Classic start of its patrol in the lower halls. Use the stairs from the entrance to get to the upper defenders or the lower statues. [Entrance and stair walkthrough](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/temple-of-atalhakkar-sunken-temple-dungeon-strategy-wow-classic)
- **Lower statues and pit** numbers the six statues in activation order: south, north, southwest, southeast, northwest, northeast. The shared north-statue and altar pin opens both entries and identifies the altar on the level below. Atal'alarion and the Idol of Hakkar are marked separately in the pit below the statue gallery. [Classic spawn and object records](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/13e933726f386399d9820138fbd1123e7cda86c8/Updates/Instances/109_sunken_temple.sql) · [Classic summon and event order](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/eastern_kingdoms/sunken_temple/sunken_temple.h)
- **Upper defender balconies** marks the six positions to clear before Jammal'an. The Classic spawn group can assign Gasher, Hukku, Loro, Mijan, Zolo, and Zul'Lor to different balconies, so the pins identify the balconies by direction. All eight equipment sources are reachable through **Dungeon drops** links on the map. [Classic defender slots and group](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/13e933726f386399d9820138fbd1123e7cda86c8/Updates/Instances/109_sunken_temple.sql)

## Swamp of Sorrows approach

The map shows the Pool of Tears ruins, the underground portal, three rares in the approach, and Itharius's cave. The underground portal pin uses the Classic return point immediately outside the instance. Underground pins are projected onto the surface map. [Entrance walkthrough](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/temple-of-atalhakkar-sunken-temple-dungeon-strategy-wow-classic)

Enable **Quest materials** to see all 15 Classic tablet positions outside the instance, five indoor examples, and two areas for collecting Atal'ai Haze. The Classic snapshot has 40 indoor tablet records across several elevations. The five indoor examples sit on identifiable map sections. Seven **Mossy Footlocker** pins show Classic reference sites around the Pool of Tears. No generic indoor treasure chest spawn coordinates were found in the inspected snapshot. [Marker and source inventory](sources.json)

## Coordinates and credits

Indoor positions use the origin and scale of each minimap image, with north up. The four comparable boss markers and the entrance arrival agree with ForeverDB within 0.02 percentage points. One marker can display several entries when different levels project to the same position. Classic reference positions may differ in the live game. Source URLs, projection checks, and image hashes are in the [source inventory](sources.json).
