# Sunken Temple

Researched November 23, 2026. **Dungeons → Sunken Temple (50–60)** uses the current Group Finder range in the full dungeon record for instance 109. The entrance is in the Pool of Tears in the Swamp of Sorrows. The tracker lists 20 Alliance/shared quests available to Rogues and 39 equipment drops across eight sources. [Dungeon record, build 1.60.1.71037](https://foreverdb.net/data/dungeons/109.json)

## Quests

- Brohann Caskbelly's seven-step investigation begins in Stormwind and ends with collecting ten Atal'ai Tablets. The suggested pickup is 69.5, 40.4 on the Forever Stormwind map. Some guides list 64.2, 20.8 for the same Dwarven District pickup. The current quest record lists level 38 as the minimum for Into The Temple of Atal'Hakkar. The older Classic record lists level 41. [Quest record](https://foreverchanges.pro/quest/1475/into-the-temple-of-atalhakkar) · [Brohann coordinates](https://pullcall.com/forever/db/npc/5384-brohann-caskbelly)
- The Alliance introduction from Angelas Moonbreeze leads to The Stone Circle in Tanaris. Marvon then gives Into the Depths and Secret of the Circle as two parallel quests. The altar receives the Stone Circle. The statue sequence summons Atal'alarion and leads to the Idol of Hakkar. [Dungeon quest guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/sunken-temple-quests) · [Alliance introduction](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/3445/)
- The God Hakkar links back to The Ancient Egg in the Zul'Farrak list, where the earlier Yeh'kinya steps are tracked. Haze of Evil includes its two Alliance preparation quests. Jammal'an the Prophet starts in the Hinterlands. [The God Hakkar](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=3528/the-god-hakkar) · [Haze of Evil](https://foreverchanges.pro/quest/4143/haze-of-evil)
- The Rogue quest The Azure Key starts in Azshara, requires looting the key from Morphaz, and ends with Lord Jorach Ravenholdt in Alterac Mountains. The Azshara entry uses the corrected objective. Both lists share quest ID 8236 and its completion state. The Sunken Temple introduction likewise shares ID 3445 with Feralas. [The Azure Key](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=8236/the-azure-key)
- The two Essence of Eranikus quests have separate IDs. The first starts from the Shade's drop and awards a trinket at the Essence Font. Itharius's follow-up consumes that trinket, then sends the player to Winterspring. [Itharius's hand-in](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=3374/the-essence-of-eranikus)

The [review ledger](sources.json) records 32 quest decisions. Three Horde variants and eight quests for other classes are outside the Alliance Rogue checklist. The placeholder record `<TXT> The Shriveled Heart` (2868) has no verified pickup or completion.
