# Mount Hyjal

Reviewed November 23, 2026. **Zones → Mount Hyjal (55–60)** has 150 quest entries for Alliance characters, including shared quests. The navigation label follows the Forever Group Finder. Some quests can be picked up at level 54, and the Rocket Gnomes reference lists level 52 with an unverified pickup. [Group Finder range](https://bindonforever.com/map/mount-hyjal) · [Quest data](../../data/quests.json)

The review compared 249 candidates from public catalogs and Questie’s quest, NPC, object and relationship records. The 158-entry Icy Veins catalog contributed 139 included quests. The other 19 are 18 Horde-only entries and one level-zero record, The Demon Tower, with no corroborated pickup. Eleven included quests came from other sources, including cross-zone introductions, Moonglade handoffs, Beads for Brerufa, and the first Belle’s Ring quest. The ledger also records API-only records with no corroborated outdoor pickup. [Icy Veins catalog](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/zones/mount-hyjal/) · [ForeverChanges](https://foreverchanges.pro/quests/mount-hyjal) · [QuestieDB revision](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/tree/47a4756b775e8c73687fd3066d6c13f0bc43a55e) · [Coverage ledger](sources.json)

- **Elderwild and the lower mountain.** Includes the Alliance Elderwild route, Fenwick’s rescue, Kaylaena’s deliveries, the Curious Crow and Felflick chains, and the introductions from Felwood, Azshara, the Blasted Lands, Un’Goro and the Western Plaguelands. NPCs and objects use coordinates on the map named in the row. [Alignment and access guide](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mount-hyjal-access/) · [The Grand Alliance quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=91882/the-grand-alliance)
- **Barkskin Burrow and the veteran.** Keeps both Water of Hyjal IDs, the repeatable Beads for Brerufa hand-in, and both Praise routes. Soldier’s Solace awards the item that starts Last Wishes. Rocket Gnomes has an Alliance quest record, with the live pickup still unverified. [Barkskin reputation](https://www.zockify.com/forever/reputation/barkskin-burrow/) · [Last Wishes](https://zephras.com/forever/enUS/quest/96561/) · [Rocket Gnomes](https://guide.wow4ever.se/en/quests/92094)
- **Ketharas, the Wild Gods and the summit.** Includes the Moonglade detour, all three recorded prerequisites for The Staff of the Mountain, the Blackthorne Pact fight and return to Jereman. Confirmed or authored requirements are linked as prerequisites. Observed and alternate introductions appear as earlier steps. The outdoor list excludes raid records without a corroborated outdoor pickup.
