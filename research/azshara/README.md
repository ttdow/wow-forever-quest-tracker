# Azshara

Researched November 23, 2026. **Zones → Azshara (45–55)** contains 74 Alliance and shared quests available to Rogues. The suggested range is for leveling. Individual quests in the checklist go up to level 60. [Zone level map](https://theforeverera.com/en/map/) · [Quest data](../../data/quests.json)

Open **Map** in Azshara for quest givers, objectives, and the Blackmaw Hold tunnel entrance. [Azshara map research](../azshara-map/README.md)

## Coverage

The ledger lists 127 candidates from the public indexes, QuestieDB's Azshara quest and NPC/object records, and quests with objectives in the zone. The checklist includes 41 quests from the 69-entry ForeverChanges index. The review also covers the 91-entry Legacy of Warcraft index, 80-entry PullCall index, 33-entry ForeverDB index, and 31 entries with Azshara map points on Questing Wiki. [Candidate decisions and source links](sources.json)

- **Talrendis Point and the Shattered Strand.** Includes Sentinel Dalia Sunblade's introduction from Darnassus, the Haldarr escort, Commander Truesong's Spitelash and Ravencrest quests, and Saria's chain through Ill Tidings. The Head of the Serpent requires both Preserving Eldarath and Reclaiming Ravencrest. [Dalia's quest](https://pd.zroot.it/forever/quests/98096) · [QuestieDB quest corrections](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/e3c3bc43a0b3532348d68d136d9fdf5d0cbffc1e/src/corrections/Forever/foreverQuestFixes.lua)
- **Blackmaw refugees.** Includes Ekkal, the Ursolan and Legashi quests, the Legashi Cauldron at 57.7, 21.0, and the reinforcement detour through Felwood. The authored QuestieDB faction corrections and the current quest pages support both factions, including Master Feardred and Later, Satyr. Ekkal's Legashi quests can be used to gain reputation before Neutral. The checklist includes Blackmaw Hold dungeon quests with outdoor pickups in Azshara and distinguishes the first-time Blackmaw Pouches quest (97327) from its repeatable hand-in (96246). [Timbermaw reputation and requirements](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/reputation/timbermaw-hold-quests-quartermaster-rewards) · [Cauldron observations](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/e3c3bc43a0b3532348d68d136d9fdf5d0cbffc1e/src/corrections/Forever/traces/foreverObjectTraces.lua)

Coordinates refer to the pickup and use the named area's map. Separate turn-ins are recorded in objectives. [The Azure Key](https://foreverdb.net/quest-chain/the-azure-key) · [Tymor's runes](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/3449/)

For Demon Hunter's Hunter, player traces record Loramus as a giver and a published listing also identifies Fel-Tainted Remains as a possible starter.
