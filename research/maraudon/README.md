# Maraudon

Researched November 17, 2026. Added under **Dungeons**, suggested levels **46–55**, with **10 Alliance/shared quest entries** and **34 boss-specific equipment drops across nine cards**. **23 items** pass the Rogue equipment filter. Maintained records: [quests.json](../../data/quests.json) and [drops.json](../../data/drops.json).

Sources: the [current quest index](https://foreverchanges.pro/quests/maraudon), individual current quest pages, [ForeverDB's full dungeon data](https://foreverdb.net/data/dungeons/349.json) at build **1.60.1.70938**, Questie's primary quest/NPC data and player traces at revision `e1652ce62113c152da682baf2d6fb85f1396bcfb`, the [All The Things dungeon record](addon.lua) at revision `36684dda55e4aa297de3ea24475a77687b54c620`, current Wowhead Forever item tooltips, and the read-only CMaNGOS Classic snapshot released November 13, 2026. Classic references and observed chain order retain their provenance.

## Quest coverage

All **13** current-index quests were checked: **nine eligible** and **four Horde-only**. The direct introduction **Word of the Woods (93594)** brings the tracker to ten entries. The [15-candidate ledger](sources.json) also rejects unrelated **Rite of Vision (771)**, an early Mulgore quest incorrectly appearing in the dungeon registry. Its presence is not evidence of a Maraudon objective.

Included:

- Twisted Evils (7028).
- Alliance Vyletongue Corruption (7041), including the vial and eight healed plants.
- Legends of Maraudon (7044) and The Scepter of Celebras (7046).
- Alliance Corruption of Earth and Seed (7065).
- Seed of Life (7066).
- The Pariah's Instructions (7067).
- Alliance Shadowshard Fragments (7070).
- Word of the Woods (93594) and Rot and Regret (93404).

Excluded Horde-only quests: Vyletongue Corruption (7029), Corruption of Earth and Seed (7064), Shadowshard Fragments (7068), and An'she's Radiance (96776). No additional Rogue-specific candidate was found in active primary category/objective and generated Forever scans. **Archmage Tervosh is a giver name, not a Mage class requirement.** His Alliance Shadowshard Fragments remains included.

Word of the Woods and Rot and Regret reuse the exact existing Shen'dralas records, changing only their list. Their existing completion IDs are shared across Shen'dralas, Razorfen Downs, and Maraudon. Forlorn Gardens and Passing the Blame link to their existing lists; the entire Razorfen Downs chain is not duplicated in Maraudon. Questie records these handoffs as observed offer order, so they remain earlier/later links rather than mandatory prerequisites.

**The Scepter of Celebras explicitly requires Legends of Maraudon** in the primary Classic quest table, corroborated by the Forever addon. Its reverse unlock link is generated by the app. **Seed of Life has no recorded Corruption of Earth and Seed completion prerequisite**: Zaetar's Spirit appears after Princess Theradras is defeated, while the separate Princess quest is turned in outside. The tracker describes the spawn condition without inventing that turn-in requirement.

The neutral Pariah quest has no Gelkis/Magram allegiance requirement recorded and remains relevant to the user's Gelkis route. Its Amulet of Union combines the five Khan gems, using the Amulet of Spirits from the Nameless Prophet. Legends identifies the Rod from Noxxion and Diamond from Lord Vyletongue; those are quest components, not equipment drops.

Pickups remain in their actual areas: Desolace, Theramore, and Shen'dralas where applicable. Outdoor coordinates are approximate published Classic references, not new Forever measurements. Cavindra is labeled in the entrance caves; the roaming Pariah coordinate is explicitly approximate. Celebras and Zaetar's Spirit have no invented outdoor coordinates.

All **34** current Forever tooltips explicitly name the corresponding boss. **Nine** also have player-recorded sources in ForeverDB and use **Seen in Forever**; the other 25 use **Wowhead ref.** Every item's stats/effects, quality, item level, and required level use the inspected current tooltip, separately marked `statsEvidence: "wowhead"`. Full tooltip payloads are preserved in [item-tooltips.json](item-tooltips.json).

The registry's eight-boss list omits **rare Meshlok**, and its loot list omits **Blade of Eternal Darkness (17780)**. The current tooltips and Forever addon corroborate Meshlok's Nature's Embrace, Fungus Shroud Armor, and Bloomsprout Headpiece, and the Princess's epic dagger. They are included rather than treating the registry as an exhaustive boundary. No generic green/world equipment is included.

The Rogue filter means equipable after training, including caster-stat equipment and the held off-hand accessory. It excludes mail, plate, the shield, wand, and two-handed weapons. It does not imply every included item has useful Rogue stats. The Blade retains its current epic quality and spell proc. Helm of the Mountain retains its separately listed base and bonus armor. Internal millisecond proc cooldowns and observed percentages are not presented as player-facing drop guarantees.

Quest items/components, quest rewards, Heart of the Wild, consumables, recipes, and ordinary world/zone equipment are excluded. The registry's Well Stone is a quest item, not equipment. Source labels remain optional and cannot block loading.
