# Maraudon map

Open **Dungeons → Maraudon → Map** for 54 searchable entries across the dungeon and its Desolace approach. The three indoor views use the original Forever client minimap sections served by ForeverDB. The approach uses the Desolace client map and links to a detailed Classic plan of the caves. [Map data](../../data/maps/maraudon.json) · [Client minimaps](https://foreverdb.net/data/dungeons/349.json) · [Entrance-cave floor plan](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/wow/maraudon/maraudon_lr.pdf#page=5)

## Dungeon interior

- The map includes all nine boss and rare encounters in Drops. **Dungeon interior** shows the two wing entrances, Celebras, and the route down to Princess Theradras and Rotgrip. **Landslide caverns** shows the separate minimap section for Landslide. Meshlok's pin marks his Classic spawn by Poison Falls. [Classic instance records](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/09000ea8d7333856727204e4e2d30f772149b7a8/Updates/Instances/349_maraudon.sql)
- Search **Legends of Maraudon** for Cavindra, the Celebrian Rod and Diamond drops, and the hand-in to Celebras the Redeemed. The nearby **Incantation of Celebras** pin marks the ritual book for the Scepter quest. The map shows both ends of the shortcut to Earth Song Falls. [Instance script](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/09000ea8d7333856727204e4e2d30f772149b7a8/Updates/Instances/349_maraudon.sql) · [Quest research](../maraudon/README.md)
- All **12 Vylestem Vines** in the Classic records can be found on the map. Five sit on the **Orange tunnel detail** view and seven on the main interior. Fill the Coated Cerulean Vial at the orange crystal pool outside the Orange portal before treating eight vines inside. [Forever quest walkthrough](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/maraudon-dungeon-overview-location-rewards)
- In the inner caverns, the **Vial of Earth Song Water** pin shares Rotgrip's pool marker. Players report the water spout under the stone bridge toward the east side of the pool. **Zaetar's Spirit** shares the Princess's arena marker and appears after her defeat. [Rot and Regret player reports](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93404/rot-and-regret#comments) · [Seed of Life research](../maraudon/README.md)

## Desolace and entrance caves

The approach view places the stone doors, both colored portals, the shortcut chamber, Cavindra, the Nameless Prophet, the Shadowshard Fragment area, the orange pool, and four outdoor quest givers on the Desolace surface map. Underground locations project onto the surface image. The Classic cave plan shows the corridors and stairs between them. Search **The Pariah's Instructions** to find the Prophet and all five Khans in both map sections. Their pins are Classic spawn references, and some roam. [Classic cave plan](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/wow/maraudon/maraudon_lr.pdf#page=5) · [Forever quest research](../maraudon/README.md)

The eight **Solid Chest** pins are the possible surface locations in the Valley of Spears pool. The Classic configuration selects one at a time. The inspected instance records supply no interior treasure chest position. Live Forever chest locations remain unverified. [Classic spawn snapshot](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/releases/tag/latest)

## Coordinates and credits

Interior coordinates use each image's origin and scale. Pixel X is `(originY − worldY) × pixelsPerYard` and pixel Y is `(originX − worldX) × pixelsPerYard`. The eight boss pins with a ForeverDB reference agree within 0.02 percentage points. The Desolace view projects the positions using the Forever client zone rectangle. Source hashes and map dimensions are listed in the [source inventory](sources.json).
