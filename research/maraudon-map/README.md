# Maraudon map

Open **Dungeons → Maraudon → Map** for 55 searchable entries. The three dungeon sections use original Forever client minimaps. **Entrance caves** uses the illustrated pre-instance map from the Train of Soul guide. **Desolace approach** uses the Desolace client map. [Map data](../../data/maps/maraudon.json) · [Client minimaps](https://foreverdb.net/data/dungeons/349.json) · [Train of Soul guide](https://www.swaziloo.com/wow/mara.html)

## Dungeon interior

- The map includes all nine boss and rare encounters in Drops. **Dungeon interior** shows the two wing entrances, Celebras, and the route down to Princess Theradras and Rotgrip. **Landslide caverns** shows the separate minimap section for Landslide. Meshlok's pin marks his Classic spawn by Poison Falls. [Classic instance records](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/09000ea8d7333856727204e4e2d30f772149b7a8/Updates/Instances/349_maraudon.sql)
- Search **Legends of Maraudon** for Cavindra, the Celebrian Rod and Diamond drops, and the hand-in to Celebras the Redeemed. The nearby **Incantation of Celebras** pin marks the ritual book for the Scepter quest. The map shows both ends of the shortcut to Earth Song Falls. [Instance script](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/09000ea8d7333856727204e4e2d30f772149b7a8/Updates/Instances/349_maraudon.sql) · [Quest research](../maraudon/README.md)
- All **12 Vylestem Vines** in the Classic records can be found on the map. Five sit on the **Orange tunnel detail** view and seven on the main interior. Fill the Coated Cerulean Vial at the orange crystal pool outside the Orange portal before treating eight vines inside. [Forever quest walkthrough](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/maraudon-dungeon-overview-location-rewards)
- In the inner caverns, the **Vial of Earth Song Water** pin shares Rotgrip's pool marker. Players report the water spout under the stone bridge toward the east side of the pool. **Zaetar's Spirit** shares the Princess's arena marker and appears after her defeat. [Rot and Regret player reports](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93404/rot-and-regret#comments) · [Seed of Life research](../maraudon/README.md)

## Entrance caves and Desolace approach

**Entrance caves** shows the corridors and stairs between the stone doors, both colored portals, the Celebras shortcut, and the orange crystal pool. It also marks Cavindra, the Nameless Prophet, the three Khans outside the instance, and the Shadowshard Fragment area. Search **The Pariah's Instructions** to find the Prophet and all five Khans across the cave and dungeon views. [Pre-instance map](https://www.swaziloo.com/wow/image_mara3.jpg) · [BradyGames cave plan](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/wow/maraudon/maraudon_lr.pdf#page=5) · [Forever quest research](../maraudon/README.md)

**Desolace approach** shows the stone doors, four outdoor quest givers, and eight possible **Solid Chest** locations in the Valley of Spears pool. The Classic configuration selects one chest location at a time. Live Forever chest locations remain unverified. [Classic spawn snapshot](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/releases/tag/latest)

## Coordinates and credits

Interior coordinates use each image's origin and scale. Pixel X is `(originY − worldY) × pixelsPerYard` and pixel Y is `(originX − worldX) × pixelsPerYard`. The eight boss pins with a ForeverDB reference agree within 0.02 percentage points. The Desolace view projects positions using the Forever client zone rectangle. Cave pins use landmarks on the community illustration. The Nameless Prophet and Shadowshard Fragment pins mark approximate areas. Classic world coordinates still identify the corresponding spawn or portal references in the data. [Source inventory](sources.json)
