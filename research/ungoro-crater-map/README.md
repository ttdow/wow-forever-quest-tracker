# Un'Goro Crater map

Open **Zones → Un'Goro Crater → Map**. The map links **54 tracked quests** at local pickups, hand-ins, and objectives. It marks Marshal's Refuge and its flight path, the three crystal pylons, the Gorishi hive, Fire Plume Ridge, and Torwa's Damaged Blue Crystal. Nearby quest givers and the raft and pack share pins where they would otherwise overlap. Each popup gives the individual reference coordinates. [Open map](https://ttdow.github.io/wow-forever-quest-tracker/#zone=Un%27Goro+Crater&view=map) · [Map data](../../data/maps/ungoro-crater.json)

## Rares and treasure

The **Rares** layer shows 18 representative positions for Ravasaur Matriarch, Clutchmother Zavas, Gruff, King Mosh, and Uhk'loc. The positions are selected from Wowhead's 320 Forever map coordinates. Each selection starts near the Classic spawn anchor and adds well-spaced positions to show where the rare has been listed across the zone. Spawn cycles and patrol paths remain unverified. [Source inventory](sources.json)

The **Ornate Chest** at **50.2, 50.0** holds the Golden Flame for Linken's quest. The audit found no verified ordinary treasure chest spawns. Six Solid Chest records within the map rectangle fall outside the client zone's terrain boundary. The Forever Era chest layer and Questie's object database also lack ordinary chest positions for Un'Goro. [Ornate Chest](https://nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/object/164910) · [Golden Flame quest](https://foreverchanges.pro/quest/3962/its-dangerous-to-go-alone) · [Chest audit](sources.json)

Enable **Quest materials** for 118 representative Un'Goro Dirt Pile and Power Crystal positions, covering all four crystal colors. Optional gathering sites use Classic locations from Questie's Forever object database and may vary in game. [Questie object records](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/0e6f3386b2a31b76b1b103e1d3aa6f4f6a7797ba/data/Forever/foreverObjectDB.lua)
