# Dire Maul East

The tracker lists **Dire Maul East (54–60)** under Dungeons, using the recommended level range published for the Warpwood Quarter in Feralas. The November 27, 2026 review accounts for 59 quest candidates from the public Dire Maul indexes, QuestieDB's Forever records, and wing-specific addon records. [ForeverChanges wing guide](https://foreverchanges.pro/dungeons/dire-maul-east) · [ForeverDB dungeon and wing ranges](https://foreverdb.net/dungeon/429-dire-maul) · [Candidate decisions](sources.json)

Seven entries cover Pusillin, Alliance Lethtendris's Web and its optional Feathermoon introduction, Shards of the Felvine, the Rogue Garona book, the Rogue-accessible right-piece Isalien branch, and In Demand - Ancient Elven Curiosities. The latter shares completion with Stranglethorn Vale and requires one item from each Dire Maul wing. The Garona book shares completion with the Rogue list. The Felvine quest links back to A Reliquary of Purity in Silithus. Bodley's More Components of Importance links to an external quest guide. [Quest data](../../data/quests.json) · [Wing and class records](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/789b0feb395396c181999063b691f854167595c6/.contrib/.db/forever/dungeons%20%26%20raids/dire%20maul.lua)

The addon calls Feathermoon Stronghold a breadcrumb, and the Classic server data records no completion prerequisite for Lethtendris's Web. The tracker shows the breadcrumb under Earlier in chain. The Quel'Serrar chain requires a Warrior/Paladin starter item. The Isalien left-piece branch belongs to Druids/Warlocks. The Horde web route, other class books, and quests specific to the other wings are excluded. The seasonal A Light in Dark Places entry awaits confirmation of its bonfire's wing. [Classic quest table snapshot](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/releases/download/latest/classic-sqlite-db.zip) · [Compendium tooltip](https://nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/item/18401)

Drops has **33 pieces of equipment in seven expandable cards**, with **18 items** in the Rogue filter. Two current equipment drops belong to Pusillin. Pimgib's Collar has its own source card because the tooltip names Pimgib, even though a player log groups it with Lethtendris. Isalien's card identifies her as summoned. Twenty-six items have player reports and the remaining seven have current matching tooltip sources. Generic world equipment, zone drops, quest items, keys, and recipes are excluded. The current Ironweave Gloves tooltip restricts them to Priest, Mage, and Warlock.
